Full Squish

A mobile app helping mountain bikers optimize their suspension setups through experimentation and data tracking

Role Product Manager / Developer
Timeline [Add Timeline]
Platform iOS Mobile

Overview

Full Squish is a mobile app designed to help mountain bikers optimize their suspension setups through experimentation and data tracking. By allowing riders to record and analyze their suspension settings, Full Squish empowers users to find the perfect balance between performance and comfort, tailored to their specific riding conditions and preferences.

The Problem

Challenge

When setting up a mountain bike, suspension configuration is critical to performance and comfort. This setup varies based on trail conditions and riding style preferences. As riders evolve their skills or explore new terrain, they need different suspension settings for optimal performance. Many riders struggle to remember their preferred settings for various conditions, leading to inconsistent experiences and reduced enjoyment.

Beyond initial factory suggestions, suspension setup is intimidating. There is a fear that you'll mess something up by changing it. If you don't play around you won't know if the factory setting is the best setting or worst setting. Then comes the issue of remember what changes you did and how that affected your ride.

  • Intimidation of changing suspension settings
  • Difficulty remembering specific settings for different conditions
  • Lack of data to compare different setups

Development Process

Development strategy was to make sure Full Squish was intuitive for users and easy to get the important information. I wanted to make sure Full Squish addressed the first problem well, which was having a away to see your suspension settings in one place, quickly. The next issue was to be able to make adjustments to your bike and save notes, and make sure that is intuitive and easy. No point in making features that are so complex people don't use them.

Technical Approach

Since I was coding with AI I needed to be very detailed and specific with the prompts. I treated every prompt as a user story and made sure it was clear and specific. I also made sure to include the full context of the app and the user's goals. I also prompted the AI to teach me what we were doing when we were coding and why we were doing it. I want to make sure I learn as a developer and not just get the job done, so I want to address any issues like not having a common asset library. So this will be a major focus going forward as well as getting new features developed.

Iterations & Testing

Before official launch, Full Squish was in beta for several months ironing out bugs and user experience. I invited locatl mountain bikes and friends to the TestFlight to get their immediate feedback. I've also incorpated user feedback into the app to continue to improve the user experience.

Since the feedback loop was short and I was heavily involved with thesting the app as well, iterations were short, fixes were quick, and new features were pushed sooner.

Future Roadmap

Full Squish is still version 1.0.1 so there are some tech debt I'd like to address and then incorporate features user could value

  • Ensure that the app is running off a design system of shared assets
  • Add a Learning Section for new riders
  • AI Modal that will help you dial in your suspension
